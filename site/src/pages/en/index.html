Zajil Download Manager

A free and open-source download manager, Arabic first, with English too. It splits a file over several connections, resumes what was cut off, downloads videos in the quality you choose, and keeps your downloads in scheduled queues.

What Zajil does

Arabic first

The whole interface runs right to left in Arabic, and left to right in English, in light and dark modes. Error messages come in your language.

Many connections

Up to 32 connections for one file, and when a connection is free it splits the slowest part. Downloads resume after the network drops or the program closes, and HTTPS certificates are always checked.

Videos and playlists

The “Video quality” window shows each quality with its size, and “Sound only”. YouTube is supported, with whole playlists or the videos you pick from one. Videos protected by DRM are not downloaded.

Browser extension

For Chrome, Edge and Firefox: it hands the browser's downloads to Zajil, adds “Download with Zajil” to the right-click menu, and a “Download video” button over videos.

Queues and scheduling

Put downloads in queues, and set when each starts and stops, how many run at once, and what happens when it ends, such as shutting the computer down.

Private, on your device

No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. Zajil connects only to the sites of the files you download, to GitHub to learn of a new version (you can turn that off), and to download the video tools once you agree. Privacy policy.

Free and open source

Under GPL-3.0-or-later, with no ads and no paid features. All the code is on GitHub, and the installers are built automatically from the published code, with a checksum for each file.

And more

“Site grabber”, “Batch download”, the “Drop box”, speed limits, categories by file type each with its own folder, and the zajil command line.
