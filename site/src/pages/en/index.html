A free, open-source download manager

Your file arrives whole, however far it comes from

Zajil splits a file into parts and downloads them at the same time, and goes on from where it stopped if the connection drops. Its interface is Arabic first, right to left, with English too, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Named after the homing pigeon

Zajil is Arabic for the homing pigeon, which carried messages however far they had to go. That is the name's promise: your file gets to you whole and sound, even if the connection breaks on the way.

Watch the file arrive

Zajil splits a file into parts and opens a connection for each, up to 32. When a connection finishes its part, it takes half of what is left of the slowest part, so no connection sits idle.

Connection dropped? Nothing is lost

Zajil keeps what arrived as it goes. If the connection drops, or you close the program, the download goes on from where it stopped. If the link expires, give it a new link to the same file with “Refresh link”.

A sound file, never a mix

Before it goes on, Zajil makes sure the file on the server has not changed, so old and new content never mix.

Every download in its place

From choosing a video's quality to scheduling downloads for the night, all in one clear window.

Videos in the quality you choose, and whole playlists

The “Video quality” window shows each quality there is with its size, and “Sound only”. YouTube is supported, with a whole playlist or the videos you pick from it, and sound and picture are merged when they come apart.

The video tools are open source (yt-dlp, FFmpeg and QuickJS-NG), downloaded once when you agree. Zajil never gets around content protection: if a video is protected by DRM, it tells you so plainly.

  • Queues

    Put downloads in queues, and set how many run at once and how many times each is retried.

  • Scheduling by hour and day

    Choose when to start, when to stop, and on which days. Zajil can shut the computer down or put it to sleep when the queue ends.

  • Speed limits

    Limit the whole program or each download on its own, so your other work keeps enough speed.

  • Automatic categories

    Zajil puts each file in its category: Programs, Video, Music, Documents and Archives. Each has its own folder, and rules you can change.

Truly Arabic, and English in one click

Zajil's interface was built right to left from the start, not translated afterwards. And it switches to English in one click, without restarting the program.

FAQ

All questions

Is Zajil really free?

Yes, completely free. There is no paid version, no locked features and no ads.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11, macOS 11 or later, and Linux (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or later). There is no phone app.

Why does a warning show on first run?

Because Zajil is not signed by Apple or Microsoft. How to open it is on the download page, with how to check the file's checksum.

How do I connect Zajil to my browser?

Install Zajil and start it once, then add its extension to Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Downloads then go straight to Zajil, by the file types you choose. The steps.

Does Zajil collect any data about me?

No. No tracking, no analytics, no ads. The details are in the privacy policy.
